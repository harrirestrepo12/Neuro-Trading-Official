## About Neuro Trading

Neuro Trading is an AI-powered trading technology platform designed to integrate financial market analysis, structured risk management, MetaTrader 5 connectivity and trading automation.

Our objective is to provide traders with an integrated environment for monitoring markets, analyzing information and managing trading workflows.

## Platform Capabilities

- AI-assisted market analysis
- MetaTrader 5 integration
- Real-time market monitoring
- Structured risk management
- Trading automation workflows
- Broker connectivity
- Interactive trading dashboards
- Execution safeguards
